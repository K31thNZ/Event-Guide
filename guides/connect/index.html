The Moscow expat community

Smaller than before 2022, but tighter, more resourceful, and more self-reliant. Here's how to find your people and get the most out of being here.

The English-speaking expat community in Moscow has contracted since 2022, but those who remain are — without exception — people who chose to be here. That makes for a more genuine, more connected, and more useful community than many larger expat scenes elsewhere. New arrivals are welcomed, shared knowledge is valued, and the network is genuinely helpful.
