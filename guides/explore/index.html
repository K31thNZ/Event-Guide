Exploring Moscow

The resident's guide to one of the world's most underrated cities for expats. Moscow rewards those who go beyond the guidebook — here's how.

🏙️

Moscow as a resident, not a tourist

Most guides treat Moscow as a short-stay destination. As a resident you have something far better — time. The metro alone is worth weeks of exploration. The restaurant scene is world-class and affordable. The cultural calendar is relentless. This section is written for people who live here.

Expat community picks — updated regularly

Best casual dinner

Khachapuri — Georgian food, impossible to go wrong. Any of the Khinkali chains for dumplings.

Best coffee

Double B Coffee has consistently excellent espresso. Shokoladnitsa for a local café experience.

Most underrated museum

The Pushkin Museum of Fine Arts. Consistently overlooked — extraordinary European collection.

Best metro station to visit

Komsomolskaya — the chandeliers and mosaics are genuinely extraordinary. A heritage site underground.
