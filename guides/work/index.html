The most useful route for skilled professionals

Highly Qualified Specialist (HQS) status

Russia's HQS programme offers a streamlined work permit, simplified tax rate (flat 13% regardless of residency status), and significant other benefits for foreign professionals earning above the threshold salary. It is the most favourable work status for skilled expats and the most common route for those on corporate postings.
