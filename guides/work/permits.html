Work permits for foreign nationals — 2026

The HQS programme is the main route for Western expats. Here's how it works, what the September 2026 threshold changes mean, and the step-by-step process.

Updated June 2026 10 min read

Legal note: Work permit rules change frequently. This guide reflects the position as of June 2026. Important: A significant increase to HQS salary thresholds was passed in first reading by the State Duma on 18 March 2026 and is proposed to take effect from 1 September 2026. If you are planning to apply for or renew an HQS permit, verify the current threshold before proceeding. Contact us for referral to an immigration specialist.

Working in Russia as a foreign national without the correct permit is a serious legal offence with consequences including fines, deportation, and bans on future entry. There are two main routes: the standard work permit (квота-зависимый), and the Highly Qualified Specialist programme (HQS / ВКС). Almost all Western expats use the HQS route.

The HQS programme — the main route for Western expats

Russia's Highly Qualified Specialist (HQS / ВКС — Высококвалифицированный Специалист) programme is designed to attract skilled foreign professionals. It offers a streamlined work permit, significant tax advantages, and greater flexibility than the standard quota route. The employer decides whether the specialist qualifies — there is no government assessment of skills; qualification is entirely salary-based.

HQS salary threshold — important 2026 update

The HQS salary threshold has historically been the key qualifying criterion. As of early 2026, the current threshold under Section 13.2 of the Federal Law "On the Rights of Foreign Citizens" requires a minimum salary of ₽750,000 per calendar quarter (₽250,000/month) for most HQS workers.

However, on 18 March 2026, a bill proposing a sharp increase to the minimum salary requirement passed its first reading in the State Duma. The proposed threshold — while the exact final number is subject to amendment — represents a near-tripling of the current level and would take effect from 1 September 2026. Contact us for the latest confirmed figure before applying.

Existing HQS employees whose salaries fall below the new threshold after 1 September 2026 will not be able to continue working under the HQS regime. Employers will need to either increase salaries or transition affected employees to alternative permit categories before the deadline.

HQS benefits in detail

13% income tax from day one

The most financially significant benefit. Standard foreign employees pay 30% income tax until they achieve 183 days of Russian residence. HQS employees pay 13% from their first day of employment, regardless of residency status. On a ₽300,000/month salary, this saves ₽51,000/month during the first six months alone.

3-year permit, no quota

HQS permits are issued for up to 3 years and are not subject to regional employment quotas — unlike standard work permits, which are quota-restricted and can be refused even if the employer wants to hire you. Renewals are possible without leaving Russia.

Family members

HQS family members receive residence permits for the duration of the HQS permit. Medical examinations for family members are only required once every 3 years (rather than annually for standard permits). Family members do not need separate work permits to live in Russia.

Multi-regional coverage

HQS permits can be issued to cover multiple Russian federal regions simultaneously, unlike standard permits which are region-specific. HQS employees and dependents also have 30 days to re-register when moving between regions, rather than the standard 7 days.

HQS application process — step by step

  1. Secure a job offer from a Russian employer authorised to hire under the HQS programme. The employer must not have violated employment laws for foreign nationals in the previous two years.
  2. Employer submits the application to the MVD (Ministry of Internal Affairs). The employer files on your behalf — you cannot self-apply. The employer submits the employment contract, your documents, and an invitation request.
  3. Work permit issued — approximately 21 business days after application. The work permit and work visa invitation are prepared simultaneously. The permit fee is ₽4,200 (statutory duty, unchanged under 2026 proposals).
  4. Obtain your HQS work visa at the Russian embassy in your home country (or your current country of residence), using the invitation issued with the permit.
  5. Arrive in Russia and register at your address within 7 days. Undergo a medical examination at an approved clinic within 30 days of the permit decision.
  6. Employer notifies tax authority within 10 days of your arrival. You receive your INN (tax number). Your employer then files quarterly salary notifications to the MVD.
Collect your permit on time: You must collect your work permit within 30 calendar days of the decision to issue it. If you fail to collect it on time, the decision is cancelled and you and your dependents must leave Russia within 15 days. There is one possible extension of 30 days if you have a valid reason — request it from the employer.

Standard work permit (quota route)

For roles that do not meet the HQS salary threshold, a standard quota-based work permit is required. This route is significantly more complex: the employer must obtain a quota allocation (quotas are set nationally and by region, and may be exhausted), and the permit is region-specific, valid for one year, and requires an annual medical examination. The tax rate of 30% applies until the employee reaches 183 days of Russian residence. Most Western expats and their employers aim for the HQS route to avoid these complications.

Path to permanent residence via HQS

HQS status opens a faster route to a Temporary Residence Permit (РВП) and eventually Permanent Residence (ВНЖ). From January 2024, HQS employees can apply for a standard permanent residence permit after completing the required period. The pathway: HQS work permit → Temporary Residence Permit (РВП) → Permanent Residence (ВНЖ). The specific timeline depends on continuous employment and meeting salary requirements throughout.
