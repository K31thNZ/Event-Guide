T-Bank is currently the easiest for foreigners to open (June 2026)

Went through the process last week. T-Bank (formerly Tinkoff) was the smoothest — courier-delivered card, English app, and they accepted my UK passport with no extra fuss. Sberbank asked for more documents. Your mileage may vary but worth trying T-Bank first.

Banking June 2026

Khachapuri na Arbate — still the best value Georgian food in central Moscow

Been here 4 years and this place has never let me down. The Adjarian khachapuri is enormous and costs around ₽680. No English menu but Google Translate camera does the job. Go before 7pm to avoid the queue.

Food Arbat

For school enrolment at ISM — apply now for September, don't wait

We tried to enrol our daughter in Year 4 in August. Waiting list for September. Had to go with a temporary Russian school for a term while waiting. The ISM admissions team told us they have spaces now for Sept 2026. Don't leave it.

Schools Time-sensitive

MFC on Beregovoy Proyezd is the fastest for registration paperwork

Tried three different MFC locations for my re-registration after returning from a trip. The one on Beregovoy Proyezd (near Fili metro) had no queue at 11am on a Wednesday and the staff were patient with my Russian. Took 25 minutes total.

Registration Admin

Informal Russian/English language exchange at Coffee 22 on Nikitsky Bulvar every Wednesday evening. Mix of students, young professionals, and a few expats. Free, just turn up. Great way to practice Russian and meet locals.
