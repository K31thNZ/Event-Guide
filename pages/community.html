MFC on Beregovoy Proyezd — fastest for registration paperwork

Tried three different MFC locations for re-registration after returning from a trip. The one on Beregovoy Proyezd (near Fili metro) had no queue at 11am on a Wednesday and the staff were patient with my broken Russian. Took 25 minutes total including photocopying. The branch on Novoslobodskaya had a 2-hour wait on the same day.

T-Bank currently the easiest Russian account for foreigners to open (June 2026)

Went through the process last week. T-Bank (formerly Tinkoff) was the smoothest experience — app-based, courier delivered the card, English-language app, and they accepted my UK passport plus migration registration with no extra hoops. Sberbank asked for an INN on top, which I didn't have yet. Alfa-Bank was second choice. Your mileage may vary but worth trying T-Bank first.

Khachapuri na Arbate — still the best value Georgian food in central Moscow

Been here 4 years and this place has never let me down. The Adjarian khachapuri is enormous and costs around ₽680. Khinkali (dumplings) are ₽75–90 each and you need at least five. No English menu but Google Translate camera works perfectly on theirs. Go before 7pm to avoid the queue — it builds fast after 19:30. Staff are friendly even if you don't speak Russian.

ISM September enrolment — apply now, don't wait until August

We tried to enrol our daughter in Year 4 in August last year. Waiting list for September. Had to use a temporary Russian school for a term while waiting. The ISM admissions team told me they currently have spaces for September 2026. Don't leave it. The British system means you really can't easily skip a year if you miss the intake.

Yandex Taxi surge pricing tip — order 3–4 minutes before you leave

If you order the moment you need it during peak hours you'll often get a 1.8–2.2x surge. If you order 3–4 minutes before you're ready to go and watch the price, it frequently drops back to normal rate within a minute or two. Doesn't always work but saves money about 60% of the time. Also: the "economy" tier often gives the same car as "comfort" — try economy first.

Language exchange at Coffee 22 — Wednesdays 19:00, just turn up

Informal Russian/English language exchange at Coffee 22 on Nikitsky Bulvar every Wednesday evening. Mix of students, young professionals and a few expats. Completely free, no registration needed. Great way to practice Russian and actually meet Muscovites rather than just other expats. I've been going three months and my Russian has improved more here than from my formal lessons.

Ask your landlord about registration BEFORE negotiating rent — saves wasted viewings

Spent three weeks viewing flats before understanding that registration refusal was the deal-breaker. Landlords who won't register you are common and there's no point negotiating everything else first. The agent I eventually used (recommended by this community) pre-screens landlords for registration willingness before showing flats to foreigners. Worth paying the agent fee.

ВкусВилл (VkusVill) is the best supermarket for Western-style food

If you're missing the kind of food you had at home — good cheese, quality deli meats, organic products, interesting sauces — VkusVill is the answer. Far better quality than Pyaterochka or Magnit. Prices are higher but comparable to a UK Waitrose. Their app (in Russian, use translate) lets you order for delivery in 30–60 minutes. Branches across the city including several in Patriarshiye Prudy area.
