Sunday Expat Hike

10:00 Gorky Park

Casual Sunday morning walk through Gorky Park and along the Frunzenskaya embankment. All levels welcome, 8–12 people typically. Ends at a café for brunch. A regular community staple — just turn up.

Free Outdoors Weekly

Jun

Newcomers Dinner

19:30 Khamovniki (venue TBC)

Monthly dinner for expats who've arrived in the last six months. Great for meeting people in the same position. Hosted by long-term community members who share hard-won local knowledge.

Members Monthly

Jun

Expat Football — 5-a-side

18:00 Luzhniki sports complex

Weekly 5-a-side for expats of all abilities. Mixed nationalities, no experience needed. Bibs available. Drinks at a nearby bar after. Message us to be added to the WhatsApp group for weekly updates.

Free Weekly
3
Jul

Bolshoi — English Pre-show Talk

18:30 Bolshoi Theatre, New Stage

English-language pre-show discussion covering the evening's production, key themes, and what to watch for. Tickets purchased separately — community discount code available on RSVP. Perfect for first-timers at the Bolshoi.

Ticketed Cultural
5
Jul

Expat Kids Playdate

10:30–13:00 Zaryadye Park

Monthly morning playdate for expat families with children aged 2–10. Parents can chat while kids play in one of Moscow's newest parks. Bring a snack to share. Café nearby for afterwards.

Free Ages 2–10
9
Jul

English Book Club

19:00 Nikitsky Bulvar café (TBC)

Monthly English-language book club discussing a pre-agreed title. July's book: announced two weeks before. Friendly mix of expats and English-speaking Russians. No homework required — just show up and join the conversation.

Free Monthly
12
Jul

Expat Professionals Drinks

18:30 Patriarshiye Prudy area

Quarterly networking drinks for expats working in professional roles in Moscow — finance, energy, education, tech, translation. Relaxed format, no name tags, no pitches. Good conversations guaranteed.

Professional Quarterly
13
Jul

Running Club — Izmailovo Park

08:30 Izmailovo Park, central entrance

Sunday morning run in one of Moscow's largest parks. Two pace groups: 5:30 min/km and 6:30 min/km. Usually 5–8 runners. Coffee and pastries after at the park café. Runs rain or shine.

Free All paces
